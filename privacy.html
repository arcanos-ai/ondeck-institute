Privacy notice: OnDeck Institute sign-ups

Last updated: 23 November 2026

OnDeck Marketing is a trade name of 2594663 Alberta Inc. In this notice, "OnDeck", "we" and "us" mean that company. OnDeck Institute is the name of OnDeck's demonstration school. It is not a real institution. This notice is for people who sign up on the OnDeck Institute form.

OnDeck works with OnDeck Institute to reach out to people who might want to study there. Here is what we do with your information, in plain language.

What we collect: your name, your contact details, and the answers you give us about your studies and plans.

Why we collect it: to talk with you about full-time programs starting in 2027, understand what you are looking for, and pass a short summary to OnDeck Institute if you ask us to.

Texts: we text you only if you tick the text box on the sign-up form. Saying yes is not required. How many texts you get can change. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop, or HELP for help. The texting terms are on the texting terms page.

Who processes it: OnDeck runs the calls and texts. Twilio delivers the texts. Retell processes voice calls. Microsoft Azure hosts and stores this information in Canada. Each processor only gets the data it needs to do its job.

What we do not do: we do not sell your information. We do not share your mobile number, or your agreement to receive texts, with anyone for their own marketing. We share your details with OnDeck Institute's recruitment team only if you say yes when we ask.

Where it is stored: your information is stored in Canada. An approved processor may handle a small part of it outside Canada, only if needed and only if approved.

How long we keep it: we erase the records of people who take part in an OnDeck demonstration after the demonstration ends.

Your choices: you can say no to any channel, or withdraw consent at any time. During a call, say "stop calling". For texts, reply STOP (reply HELP for help). You can also ask what we have about you.
